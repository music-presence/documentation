   Discord Presence refers to the presence on your profile on Discord indicating what you are up to. When it comes to music, you will be able 
   to show not only which song you are listening to but also the artist, album cover, and more information related to the playback process live 
   and it can show what games you are playing. This way, friends and other users will be aware of your activities and show your music taste 
   through Discord.

Music Presence makes it possible for Discord to display your music activity regardless of the service you use: the player may be any one supported by Music Presence and not necessarily Spotify.
